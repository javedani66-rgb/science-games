# Audit: can the child actually know the answer? (2026-10-01)

Teacher 08:02–08:04: «خطاهایی از این دست مطلقا قابل تحمل نیست، در هیچ مرحله‌ای.» Decide by educational-game design standards.

## The rules every challenge must pass
- **R1 Answerable.** A scored question is answerable from what is on screen (scene, numbers, the visible prompt line) or from a rule taught *before* it (word card, an earlier challenge in the same mission, the visible rule line). Text hidden behind «؟» (the `<small>` part of prompts, the formula panel when «فرمول» is off) does NOT count as taught.
- **R2 No blind prediction.** A prediction about something not yet taught is a free guess: guess (never scored) → see it happen → scored question about what was seen or why (Predict–Observe–Explain).
- **R3 Pictures obey physics.** Arrow lengths, tilts and numbers in the scene always agree (heavier → longer weight arrow, static friction = push, …).
- **R4 Hints point to usable evidence.** Never «به جنس چیزها فکر کن» when material can't be seen.
- **R5 Free tries never stall.** After 3 failed free tries: «نشانم بده».
- **R6 Everything the question is about is visible from the first moment** (teacher 10:46–10:47, «هر بازی‌ای این اشکال رو داره باید درست بشه»). No blank scene with a text-only question; objects to compare are on screen before the guess; a question about everyday tools shows those tools. Check: `tests/firstscreen.py <track> <stations>` contact sheets.
